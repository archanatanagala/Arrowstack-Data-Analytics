* Checked table relationships in Power BI Model view.

* Sales and marketing funnel data are analyzed separately because a verified lead-to-order relationship has not been established.

## 5. DAX MEASURES

The following measures were created:

1. Total Revenue
2. Average Order Value
3. Total Orders
4. Total Leads
5. Converted Leads
6. Conversion Rate

Total Revenue is calculated using order item prices and excludes freight.

Average Order Value is calculated by dividing Total Revenue by the distinct number of orders.

Total Orders counts distinct order IDs.

Total Leads counts distinct marketing qualified lead IDs.

Converted Leads counts distinct lead IDs in the closed deals dataset.

Conversion Rate is calculated by dividing Converted Leads by Total Leads.
